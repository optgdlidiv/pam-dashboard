Right routed: the train entered (receipt / taken over) or left (despatch / handed over) Delhi Division at the I/C point where the RBS shortest path crosses the DLI boundary (or within 5 stations of it). Wrong routed: the RBS path crosses DLI at another I/C point, or does not touch DLI at all. Via DFC: taken over from / handed over to WDFC or EDFC - RBS ShortPath does not cover the DFC, so these are not judged. Not checked: RBS did not accept a station code (sidings) or gave no path.
RBS ShortPath gives the shortest chargeable route; a train diverted on authority (block, DFC, rationalised route) also shows as differing from it. Source: FOIS MA4 train-wise interchange (previous day) and RBS ShortPath (rbs.indianrail.gov.in).
