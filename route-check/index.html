Wrong routed — by pattern

actual I/C used vs I/C on the RBS shortest path · click a row for its trains
Right routed: the train entered (receipt / taken over) or left (despatch / handed over) Delhi Division at the I/C point where the RBS shortest path crosses the DLI boundary (or within 5 stations of it). Wrong routed: the RBS path crosses DLI at another I/C point, or does not touch DLI at all. RBS paths include the DFC (WDFC / EDFC sections), so DFC I/C points are judged like any other. Not checked: RBS did not accept a station code (sidings) or gave no path. Tile level: High ≥ 40 % wrong, Medium 20–40 %, Low < 20 % of judged trains.
RBS ShortPath gives the shortest chargeable route; a train diverted on authority (block, rationalised route, line capacity) also shows as differing from it. Source: FOIS MA4 train-wise interchange (previous day) and RBS ShortPath (rbs.indianrail.gov.in).
